I design complex digital products — and I lead the teams and processes that bring them to life. What makes my profile unusual is a combination of three perspectives that rarely come together: a degree as a state-certified business economist (marketing & sales), training as a digital and print media designer, and several years as a Senior UX/UI Designer with team leadership responsibility. When it helps, I prototype ideas directly in code. That lets me think about products from a business, user, and implementation perspective at once — and translate between business, design, and engineering.

Leadership & Ownership

"Leadership is part of my role, not an add-on. For several years I was responsible for the professional leadership of a design team — including quality assurance and running parallel projects. Before that, as a procurement manager, I carried budget and negotiation responsibility. That combination of creative leadership and business thinking shapes how I work: structured, and in close exchange with everyone involved."
