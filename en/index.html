Focus Areas

Four disciplines,
one focus

Product Discovery & Strategy

Before an interface takes shape, I clarify what problem a product actually needs to solve — for users and for the business. User research, stakeholder alignment, and prioritizing needs produce a clear direction that every later decision gets measured against.

Complex Workflows & Configurators

Data-dense, multi-step processes — configurators for products that need explaining, or workflow systems with many roles and rules — I structure so people reach their goal reliably. The point is to order complexity, not pass it straight through to the surface.

Design Systems & Scale

I build design systems from the ground up and work within existing ones — reusable components, clear rules, a shared language. That's what keeps products consistent and maintainable across teams, markets, and releases, instead of splintering into one-off solutions.

Delivery with Engineering

A concept only earns its keep once it ships cleanly. I work closely with engineering teams — from handoff through iterative alignment to testing — so the design becomes a product people can rely on.

Approach

Problem
first.

I start by asking what problem a product actually needs to solve — for users and for the business. That clarity is what shapes structure, interface, and priorities. I stay in close step with product and engineering, and sharpen the solution iteratively until it holds up in numbers.
